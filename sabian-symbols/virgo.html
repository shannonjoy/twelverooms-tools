Virgo: all 30 Sabian symbols

Every whole degree of Virgo has its own Sabian symbol, a small traditional picture first recorded in 1925. Find the exact degree of your Sun, Moon, Ascendant, or any planet in Virgo, and read what that degree adds to it.

Virgo 1°

A portrait draws out and idealizes one figure's single most telling feature.

Virgo 2°

A large white cross stands raised over the land, visible from every direction.

Virgo 3°

Two guardian figures stand watch, keeping careful protection over someone.

Virgo 4°

Children of different backgrounds play together, easy and unbothered by the lines the adults around them draw.

Virgo 5°

A man becomes quietly aware of nature spirits and other unseen presences moving around him.

Virgo 6°

A merry-go-round turns, the same riders circling past the same painted horses again and again.

Virgo 7°

A household is run to look after many people at once, each given her own formal place within it.

Virgo 8°

A five-year-old takes her very first formal dancing lesson.

Virgo 9°

An artist sketches in a bold, unconventional style, reaching for a shape no one has drawn quite this way before.

Virgo 10°

Two faces look outward, their gaze reaching past the shadows just in front of them.

Virgo 11°

Virgo 12°

Just after the ceremony, the bride's veil is lifted away at last.

Virgo 13°

A steady, capable leader calms a crowd that has worked itself into panic.

Virgo 14°

A family tree spreads across the page, branch after branch of names.

Virgo 23°

An animal trainer works with a full-grown lion, holding its attention through precise, practiced skill.

Virgo 24°

A shepherd tends a single small lamb, gentle and completely attentive to it.

Virgo 25°

A flag flies at half-mast in front of a large public building.

Virgo 26°

A boy carries the incense at the altar, moving carefully through his small part of the larger ceremony.

Virgo 27°

A group of formally dressed women meets for tea, observing every rule of the occasion.

Virgo 28°

A man with a shaved, unadorned head has just taken hold of real power.

Virgo 29°

Virgo 30°
