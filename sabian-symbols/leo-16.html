Remember to round up: a planet or point at 9°30′ takes the 10° symbol, not the 9° one.

What this degree describes

Relief made visible: the storm actually ends, and the light that follows feels earned precisely because of what came before it. This degree trusts that Leo's brightness means more, and lands harder, once it has been tested by something genuinely difficult. Nothing about this sunshine is naive, it has weather behind it, and that is exactly what makes it worth celebrating rather than taking for granted.

When a planet or point falls here

A planet at this degree often shows resilience that produces real, earned brightness on the other side of difficulty. Jupiter or the Sun here can indicate someone whose optimism means the most because it survived something real. It rewards letting the relief be as loud as the storm was.
