Aries 20°

The traditional symbol for this degree, one of the 360 Sabian symbols first recorded in 1925: “A young girl feeds birds in winter.”

THE SYMBOL

“A young girl feeds birds in winter.”

Aries 20° · Sabian symbols, devised 1925 (Marc Edmund Jones, clairvoyant Elsie Wheeler)

Remember to round up: a planet or point at 9°30′ takes the 10° symbol, not the 9° one.

What this degree describes

Small, unglamorous care, offered in a season that makes it matter more. Nothing about this image is dramatic; a child, some seed, a handful of birds who would otherwise go hungry. Aries usually wants its gestures big and immediate, but here the degree insists that quiet, regular tending, done without an audience, is its own real form of strength. Winter is the setting for a reason: this is care offered precisely when conditions are hardest.

When a planet or point falls here

A planet at this degree often shows genuine nurturing instinct expressed in small, consistent acts rather than grand gestures, tending to whoever or whatever needs it most in a lean season. The Moon or Venus here can indicate someone whose care is most present exactly when things are hardest for others. It is a strong degree for steady kindness that never announces itself.

Who, or what, are you quietly feeding through their winter, without expecting anyone to notice?
