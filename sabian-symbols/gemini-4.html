Gemini 4°

The traditional symbol for this degree, one of the 360 Sabian symbols first recorded in 1925: “Sprigs of holly and mistletoe, hung again this year, bring back every Christmas that came before this one.”

THE SYMBOL

“Sprigs of holly and mistletoe, hung again this year, bring back every Christmas that came before this one.”

Gemini 4° · Sabian symbols, devised 1925 (Marc Edmund Jones, clairvoyant Elsie Wheeler)

Remember to round up: a planet or point at 9°30′ takes the 10° symbol, not the 9° one.

What this degree describes

A repeated, seasonal object doing the quiet work of time travel: each hanging is this year and, at the same time, every other year folded invisibly into it. Gemini, usually chasing the newest fact, pauses here to notice that repetition itself carries real meaning, that the tenth time you do a thing is not the same as the first, it is thick with every time in between. Ritual working quietly as memory.

When a planet or point falls here

A planet here often shows sentiment triggered by small, recurring objects or traditions, the associative mind at its most tender. The Moon at this degree favors nostalgia used well, as genuine continuity rather than avoidance. On the fourth house, it can describe a home defined more by its repeated rituals than by any single event.

What small recurring thing, hung up again this year, would bring back every year it has ever marked?
