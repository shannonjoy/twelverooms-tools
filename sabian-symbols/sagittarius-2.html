Sagittarius 2°

The traditional symbol for this degree, one of the 360 Sabian symbols first recorded in 1925: “A brisk wind stirs the open sea into scattered, restless whitecaps.”

THE SYMBOL

“A brisk wind stirs the open sea into scattered, restless whitecaps.”

Sagittarius 2° · Sabian symbols, devised 1925 (Marc Edmund Jones, clairvoyant Elsie Wheeler)

Remember to round up: a planet or point at 9°30′ takes the 10° symbol, not the 9° one.

What this degree describes

Restlessness given visible shape: the wind doesn't ask the ocean's permission, it simply moves it, and the whole surface answers at once. This is Sagittarius's native appetite for motion, meeting the vast, indifferent scale it actually likes testing itself against. Nothing about a single whitecap lasts, it rises, catches the light, and folds back under, but the sea in that moment is unmistakably, restlessly alive.

When a planet or point falls here

A planet at this degree often shows restless energy that needs a genuinely large field to move across, since small ponds will not hold it. Mars or Uranus here favors someone who only really feels like themselves once conditions get a little unpredictable. It rewards trusting motion that looks chaotic from the shore but is, up close, simply the sea being itself.

What in your life has been stirred up lately that was actually overdue for some real wind?
