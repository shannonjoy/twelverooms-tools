Common questions

What is electional astrology?

Electional astrology is the practice of choosing the right moment to begin something, by reading the position of the Moon and planets at that moment. It's the same principle used to time weddings, product launches, and important conversations: some windows carry a stronger sky than others. Every tool on this site computes those windows with the Swiss Ephemeris, the same astronomical data professional astrologers use.

Are the tools on this site free?

Yes. The natal chart, Big 3, synastry grid, Saturn return calculator, transit timeline, planetary hours, and the Moon tracker are all free, with no account required. The Twelve Rooms also writes personalized natal and electional readings for a fee, for anyone who wants a fuller reading of their own chart against the sky.

How accurate are the date and chart calculations?

Every chart and date on this site is computed with the Swiss Ephemeris, the same high-precision astronomical library used across professional astrology software. Angles like the Ascendant and Midheaven, and planetary hours, are calculated for your exact location and time, not estimated.

Do I need to know my exact birth time to use these tools?

For the Big 3 and a general natal chart, sun and moon sign only need your birth date; a known birth time sharpens the Moon and adds your rising sign and house placements. For anything angle-dependent, like planetary hours, your Saturn return, or a personalized electional reading, an exact time and location make the result far more precise.

What's the difference between the free tools and a personalized reading?

The free tools compute the sky itself, the same for everyone: the best general dates for weddings, launches, and other beginnings, and the mechanics of your own chart, its wheel, aspects, and Big 3. A personalized reading goes further, reading your full chart as a whole and weighing a chosen date or moment specifically against it, done by hand rather than by table.
