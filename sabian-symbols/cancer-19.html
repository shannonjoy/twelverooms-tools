THE SYMBOL

“A priest conducts a wedding, joining two people in front of witnesses.”

Cancer 19° · Sabian symbols, devised 1925 (Marc Edmund Jones, clairvoyant Elsie Wheeler)

Remember to round up: a planet or point at 9°30′ takes the 10° symbol, not the 9° one.

What this degree describes

A private bond gets formalized here, made public and sanctioned by ritual and community rather than kept as a purely personal understanding between two people. Cancer, usually protective of intimacy, gets a degree about the moment intimacy chooses to step into the open and ask for recognition. The priest is a stand-in for everyone else, the family, the tradition, the wider structure, that now formally witnesses and blesses what had, until this moment, belonged only to the couple.

When a planet or point falls here

A planet at this degree often shows a real need to formalize commitments, to move a bond from private understanding into public, recognized structure. Venus or Jupiter here can indicate someone for whom ritual, ceremony, and community witness genuinely deepen a relationship rather than merely decorate it. It rewards letting an important bond be seen and blessed, not just privately known.
