Leo 1°

The traditional symbol for this degree, one of the 360 Sabian symbols first recorded in 1925: “A surge of blood rushes to a man's head as ambition takes hold of him.”

THE SYMBOL

“A surge of blood rushes to a man's head as ambition takes hold of him.”

Leo 1° · Sabian symbols, devised 1925 (Marc Edmund Jones, clairvoyant Elsie Wheeler)

Remember to round up: a planet or point at 9°30′ takes the 10° symbol, not the 9° one.

What this degree describes

This is the first degree of the entire sign, and it opens with pure, undisguised force: feeling that refuses to stay contained, color rushing straight to the surface. Nothing here is subtle or hidden, this is Leo's whole nature announced at the very start, an inner fire that will not sit quietly waiting for permission. The rush is a warning as much as a portrait: intensity this immediate needs somewhere honest to go, or it becomes its own emergency. The sign that will spend thirty degrees learning to rule a room begins by first discovering it cannot yet rule its own pulse.

When a planet or point falls here

A planet at Leo 1 tends to run hot and immediate, feeling arriving before thought has caught up to explain it. Mars here often shows a temper that flares fast and burns clean rather than smoldering. The Sun at this degree can indicate a vitality so strong it needs real outlets, physical, creative, expressive, or it turns inward and works against its owner.

What in you rushes to the surface the instant you want something, and where does that heat actually want to go?
