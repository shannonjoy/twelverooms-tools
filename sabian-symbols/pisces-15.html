THE SYMBOL

“An officer drills his men through a mock assault under real, live artillery fire.”

Pisces 15° · Sabian symbols, devised 1925 (Marc Edmund Jones, clairvoyant Elsie Wheeler)

Remember to round up: a planet or point at 9°30′ takes the 10° symbol, not the 9° one.

What this degree describes

The midpoint of the sign, and it lands on the hardest kind of preparation there is: rehearsal made as real as danger itself, so that when the actual moment comes, the body already knows what to do. This degree refuses to let readiness stay theoretical. Pisces can drift toward hoping things simply work out; this degree insists that real courage gets built in advance, under conditions that do not pretend to be gentle.

When a planet or point falls here

A planet at this degree often shows genuine discipline built through realistic, high-stakes rehearsal rather than comfortable practice. Mars or Saturn here can indicate someone who trains for the worst case specifically so the real case will not overwhelm them. It rewards trusting the drill, even while the shells are actually live.
