Capricorn 11°

The traditional symbol for this degree, one of the 360 Sabian symbols first recorded in 1925: “A large flock of pheasants gathers across a private, carefully kept estate.”

THE SYMBOL

“A large flock of pheasants gathers across a private, carefully kept estate.”

Capricorn 11° · Sabian symbols, devised 1925 (Marc Edmund Jones, clairvoyant Elsie Wheeler)

Remember to round up: a planet or point at 9°30′ takes the 10° symbol, not the 9° one.

What this degree describes

Abundance here is deliberately managed, a landscape maintained specifically so that this much plenty can actually thrive in it. Capricorn's relationship to wealth shows itself plainly in this degree: it is not an accident, it is the result of real, ongoing stewardship, ground kept in exactly the condition that lets a flock this size flourish. The birds did not simply appear, the estate was built to hold them.

When a planet or point falls here

A planet at this degree often shows real capacity to build and maintain conditions where abundance can actually gather and stay, rather than pass through and leave. Jupiter or Saturn here can indicate wealth or resource that is well-tended, not merely accumulated. It rewards asking whether your own ground is actually kept well enough to hold what you hope will gather there.

What have you been quietly maintaining, well enough and long enough, that abundance has actually started to settle into it?
