THE SYMBOL

“A small, unclothed child reaches into the water, trying to catch a fish.”

Cancer 9° · Sabian symbols, devised 1925 (Marc Edmund Jones, clairvoyant Elsie Wheeler)

Remember to round up: a planet or point at 9°30′ takes the 10° symbol, not the 9° one.

What this degree describes

Pure, uninstructed effort meets an element that doesn't cooperate: the child hasn't learned yet that fish are fast, that hands alone rarely work, and reaches anyway, undiscouraged. This is Cancer at its most elemental, water as the medium of feeling itself, and a very young, very direct attempt to grasp something that lives entirely inside it. Nothing about the image says she'll succeed; everything about it says she'll try again the moment this attempt fails.

When a planet or point falls here

A planet at this degree often shows an unguarded, persistent reach into emotional depths, trying to catch and hold feelings that are, by nature, slippery and quick. The Moon or Neptune here can indicate someone who keeps reaching for emotional connection with real innocence, undeterred by how often the fish gets away. It rewards patience with your own early, clumsy attempts.
