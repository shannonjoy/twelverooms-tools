Capricorn 28°

The traditional symbol for this degree, one of the 360 Sabian symbols first recorded in 1925: “A large aviary attached to a country house hums with the singing of many contented birds.”

THE SYMBOL

“A large aviary attached to a country house hums with the singing of many contented birds.”

Capricorn 28° · Sabian symbols, devised 1925 (Marc Edmund Jones, clairvoyant Elsie Wheeler)

Remember to round up: a planet or point at 9°30′ takes the 10° symbol, not the 9° one.

What this degree describes

Structure, built with real intention, creates the exact conditions where song can flourish safely: the aviary did not happen by accident, someone designed it specifically so this much life could be contained and still be genuinely happy inside the containment. Capricorn's gift for building shows itself at its warmest here, proof that a well-made structure is not a cage, it is what actually lets abundant, singing life thrive rather than scatter.

When a planet or point falls here

A planet at this degree often shows a real talent for building structures generous enough that whoever lives inside them actually flourishes, rather than merely survives. Venus or Jupiter here can indicate someone whose careful containers produce real, contented abundance. It rewards trusting that the right structure is what makes the singing possible, not what silences it.

What structure have you built well enough that everyone inside it is actually, audibly, thriving?
