Pisces 20°

The traditional symbol for this degree, one of the 360 Sabian symbols first recorded in 1925: “A table is set for dinner, ready for the people who will gather around it.”

THE SYMBOL

“A table is set for dinner, ready for the people who will gather around it.”

Pisces 20° · Sabian symbols, devised 1925 (Marc Edmund Jones, clairvoyant Elsie Wheeler)

Remember to round up: a planet or point at 9°30′ takes the 10° symbol, not the 9° one.

What this degree describes

Preparation made for company that has not yet arrived: the places are set, the meal is ready, and the whole scene exists purely in anticipation of the people who are about to sit down together. This degree honors hospitality as its own real work, the quiet labor of getting a table ready specifically so that connection, when it comes, has somewhere comfortable to happen.

When a planet or point falls here

A planet at this degree often shows real talent for creating the conditions where genuine gathering and nourishment can happen. Venus or the Moon here can indicate someone who instinctively knows how to prepare a welcome before it is even asked for. It rewards trusting the table you have already set, instead of anxiously re-checking it.

Who have you already set a place for, that you are still waiting, a little nervously, to actually arrive?
