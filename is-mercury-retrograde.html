What each mark means

Pre-shadow. Mercury is still moving forward, but it is walking ground it will soon retrace. Themes that will matter during the retrograde often show their first edge here, so this is the time to back up, finish, and get signatures settled.

Retrograde. From Earth, Mercury appears to stop and move backward. It is the season for anything with the prefix re: revise, review, reconnect, repair. Beginnings you want to run cleanly go better after it.

Post-shadow. Mercury is direct again and recrossing the same stretch of sky for the third time. Plans firm up, delays clear, and what you revised gets its second chance.

Common questions

When is the next Mercury retrograde?

Mercury stations retrograde on October 24, 2026 at 20°59′ Scorpio and turns direct on November 13, 2026 at 5°02′ Scorpio (US Central time), with the shadow from October 4, 2026 to November 30, 2026. The one after that runs February 9, 2027 to March 3, 2027 in Pisces and Aquarius.

How many times is Mercury retrograde in 2027?

Three times: February 9, 2027 to March 3, 2027 in Pisces and Aquarius; June 10, 2027 to July 4, 2027 in Cancer and Gemini; October 7, 2027 to October 28, 2027 in Scorpio and Libra.

How long does Mercury retrograde last?

Between 20 and 24 days for every retrograde from 2026 to 2028, station to station. With the shadow on either side, the whole cycle runs about two months.

What is the Mercury retrograde shadow?

The pre-shadow begins when Mercury first reaches the degree where it will later turn direct, and the post-shadow ends when it clears the degree where it turned retrograde. Inside the shadow Mercury covers the same stretch of sky three times.

Is it Mercury retrograde in my timezone too?

Yes. A station happens at one moment for the whole Earth. Only the clock reading changes, so near midnight the date can differ by one day between, say, Chicago and London. This page shows US Central time and UTC, and the live answer above uses your own clock.
