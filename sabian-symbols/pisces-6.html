Pisces 6°

The traditional symbol for this degree, one of the 360 Sabian symbols first recorded in 1925: “A column of army officers marches past in full dress uniform.”

THE SYMBOL

“A column of army officers marches past in full dress uniform.”

Pisces 6° · Sabian symbols, devised 1925 (Marc Edmund Jones, clairvoyant Elsie Wheeler)

Remember to round up: a planet or point at 9°30′ takes the 10° symbol, not the 9° one.

What this degree describes

Order, displayed rather than merely followed: every rank visible at once, the whole hierarchy walking past in a single coordinated line. This degree gives Pisces, a sign usually described as boundaryless, a picture of real structure held with total discipline, each officer answering to the rank above and beneath in a formation that leaves nothing to individual improvisation. There is a kind of beauty in structure this complete, the sign's usual fluidity temporarily given a very firm shape.

When a planet or point falls here

A planet at this degree often shows genuine respect for rank, ceremony, and earned hierarchy, expressed outwardly rather than only privately felt. Saturn here can indicate real comfort inside a clearly ordered chain of command. It rewards recognizing that some structure, worn well, is its own kind of dignity.
