The traditional symbol for this degree, one of the 360 Sabian symbols first recorded in 1925: “Old friends gather again around a shared table, breaking bread together.”

THE SYMBOL

“Old friends gather again around a shared table, breaking bread together.”

Scorpio 10° · Sabian symbols, devised 1925 (Marc Edmund Jones, clairvoyant Elsie Wheeler)

Remember to round up: a planet or point at 9°30′ takes the 10° symbol, not the 9° one.

What this degree describes

Relationship resumed exactly where it left off, nourishment doubling as reconnection. At the close of the sign's first third, Scorpio gets a genuinely warm degree: not crisis, not intensity, just people who know each other well, gathered again, fed. Trust like this does not need to be rebuilt from nothing, it was banked long ago and simply gets picked back up the moment everyone sits back down.

When a planet or point falls here

A planet here often shows real capacity for loyal, long-running friendship that survives distance and time apart without souring. Venus or Jupiter can indicate relationships that reliably, dependably feed you whenever you return to them.
