How these dates are chosen

In electional astrology you choose the birth chart of the marriage, and the Moon leads every election. For a wedding she should be waxing, so the union grows in light, and not void of course, the coasting gap where beginnings drift. She should be applying to a benefic, moving toward a trine, sextile, or conjunction with Venus (love) or Jupiter (blessing and abundance), and away from Mars (strife) and Saturn (coldness). Venus and Mercury should be direct, Venus because she rules the marriage and Mercury because he rules the vows and the paperwork. A Moon in Taurus, her sign of exaltation, adds one more grace, as it does on November 13 and December 11.

What to avoid in 2027

The three Mercury retrogrades: Feb 9 to Mar 3, Jun 10 to Jul 4, and Oct 7 to 28. Signing vows and contracts under a backward Mercury invites revisited terms and crossed wires, so the dates above steer clear of all three. See the full Mercury retrograde 2027 guide.

The eclipse seasons: a solar eclipse on Feb 6 and lunar on Feb 20, then a cluster around Jul 18, Aug 2, and Aug 17. Astrologers keep weddings a good several days clear of eclipses, whose energy is abrupt and fated. The table names any best day within five days of one. July 28 and August 3 rate Good on the sky alone, but both sit close to the August 2 total solar eclipse, so pad them or look to January 13 or April 13.
