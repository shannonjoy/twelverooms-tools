Aries 25°

The traditional symbol for this degree, one of the 360 Sabian symbols first recorded in 1925: “A double promise.”

THE SYMBOL

“A double promise.”

Aries 25° · Sabian symbols, devised 1925 (Marc Edmund Jones, clairvoyant Elsie Wheeler)

Remember to round up: a planet or point at 9°30′ takes the 10° symbol, not the 9° one.

What this degree describes

Two commitments, made at once, and this degree does not say whether they agree with each other. A double promise can be a rich thing, one vow reinforcing another, or it can be the beginning of a real conflict, two loyalties that will eventually ask something incompatible. The degree simply names the doubling and leaves the reader to look honestly at their own promises: do they run in the same direction, or has one been made without checking on the other?

When a planet or point falls here

A planet at this degree often shows a life carrying two significant commitments at once, to two people, two paths, two versions of the self, that need regular, honest checking against each other. Mercury or Venus here can indicate real skill at holding both, or real difficulty when they start to pull apart. It rewards naming both promises out loud, at least to yourself.
