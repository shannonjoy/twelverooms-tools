THE SYMBOL

“A circle of dancers moves in ceremony, calling up the sun.”

Leo 20° · Sabian symbols, devised 1925 (Marc Edmund Jones, clairvoyant Elsie Wheeler)

Remember to round up: a planet or point at 9°30′ takes the 10° symbol, not the 9° one.

Note: the 1925 original for this degree used dated ethnic imagery; the picture above keeps its meaning in respectful, modern terms.

What this degree describes

A community moves together in ceremony, calling up the sun with practiced, deliberate steps passed down long before any of them were born. This degree honors ritual that treats the Sun, Leo's own ruler, as something worth actively courting rather than simply assumed to rise. There is real devotion in choreography this precise, a whole people trusting that attention, offered in the right form, matters to the outcome.

When a planet or point falls here

A planet at this degree often shows genuine devotion to ritual and rhythm, especially ritual connected to light, season, or the Sun itself. The Sun or Jupiter here can indicate someone who honors tradition through active, embodied practice rather than passive belief. It rewards taking your own rituals as seriously as this ceremony takes its own.
