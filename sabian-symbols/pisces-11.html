Pisces 11°

The traditional symbol for this degree, one of the 360 Sabian symbols first recorded in 1925: “A line of men walks a narrow path, each one searching for enlightenment.”

THE SYMBOL

“A line of men walks a narrow path, each one searching for enlightenment.”

Pisces 11° · Sabian symbols, devised 1925 (Marc Edmund Jones, clairvoyant Elsie Wheeler)

Remember to round up: a planet or point at 9°30′ takes the 10° symbol, not the 9° one.

What this degree describes

Seeking, made communal and physical: a whole line of travelers, each pursuing the same illumination, sharing one narrow trail because the way apparently only runs one width wide. This degree honors real spiritual discipline, walking toward understanding rather than simply waiting for it, and it is honest that the way is narrow, that this kind of seeking rarely allows for wandering off in ten directions at once.

When a planet or point falls here

A planet at this degree often shows genuine, disciplined spiritual seeking, a willingness to stay on one narrow path rather than sampling every possible route. Jupiter or Neptune here can indicate real, sustained pursuit of understanding. It rewards trusting that a path this narrow was chosen for a reason.
