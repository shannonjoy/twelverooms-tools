Leo 25°

The traditional symbol for this degree, one of the 360 Sabian symbols first recorded in 1925: “A camel crosses a vast, forbidding desert, carrying its load steadily on.”

THE SYMBOL

“A camel crosses a vast, forbidding desert, carrying its load steadily on.”

Leo 25° · Sabian symbols, devised 1925 (Marc Edmund Jones, clairvoyant Elsie Wheeler)

Remember to round up: a planet or point at 9°30′ takes the 10° symbol, not the 9° one.

What this degree describes

Endurance, built for exactly this terrain: the camel is not struggling against the desert, it is simply built to carry its load across it, steady and unbothered by conditions that would defeat almost anything else. Leo's stamina usually shows itself in a burst, but this degree honors the longer, quieter version, the capacity to keep moving across genuinely difficult, unglamorous ground for as long as the crossing takes.

When a planet or point falls here

A planet at this degree often shows real endurance across long, difficult stretches, built specifically for conditions that would exhaust most approaches. Saturn or Mars here can indicate steady capacity to keep going through genuinely hard terrain. It rewards trusting the slow, sustained crossing over any need for a dramatic sprint.
