Aquarius 17°

The traditional symbol for this degree, one of the 360 Sabian symbols first recorded in 1925: “A watchdog stands alert, guarding his master's home.”

THE SYMBOL

“A watchdog stands alert, guarding his master's home.”

Aquarius 17° · Sabian symbols, devised 1925 (Marc Edmund Jones, clairvoyant Elsie Wheeler)

Remember to round up: a planet or point at 9°30′ takes the 10° symbol, not the 9° one.

What this degree describes

Loyalty here is vigilance, not affection on display: the dog's whole purpose is attentiveness to threat, ready to act the instant it's needed and otherwise simply present. This degree honors protection as a genuine form of devotion, unglamorous and constant, the kind of care that mostly goes unnoticed precisely because it's doing its job. Aquarius can run detached, but this degree shows the sign fiercely committed to guarding something specific.

When a planet or point falls here

A planet at this degree often shows real protective instinct toward people, property, or causes, a readiness to act that doesn't require drama to stay switched on. Mars or Saturn here can indicate someone reliably alert to threats that others miss entirely. It rewards recognizing your own vigilance as care, not just as suspicion.

What, or who, have you been quietly guarding, without asking for any credit for the watching?
