Cancer: all 30 Sabian symbols

Every whole degree of Cancer has its own Sabian symbol, a small traditional picture first recorded in 1925. Find the exact degree of your Sun, Moon, Ascendant, or any planet in Cancer, and read what that degree adds to it.

Cancer 1°

An old flag is lowered from a ship's mast, and a new one raised in its place.

Cancer 2°

A figure hovers motionless on a magic carpet, taking in a vast, level landscape below.

Cancer 3°

A person bundled in fur leads a shaggy reindeer through a cold, dim northern canyon.

Cancer 4°

A hungry cat argues earnestly with the mouse she intends to catch.

Cancer 5°

A car races a train to the crossing and loses; the driver does not survive.

Cancer 6°

Wild birds gather soft material to feather their nests in the first flush of spring.

Cancer 7°

Two nature spirits dance together by moonlight.

Cancer 8°

A procession of rabbits, dressed in human clothes, walks as if on parade.

Cancer 9°

A small, unclothed child reaches into the water, trying to catch a fish.

Cancer 10°

A large diamond, still rough, in the earliest stage of being cut.

Cancer 11°

A clown makes faces, exaggerating the mannerisms of people everyone recognizes.

Cancer 12°

A woman nurses an infant whose quiet glow marks it as an old soul, returned.

Cancer 13°

A hand is held out for examination, its thumb unusually strong and prominent.

Cancer 14°

A very old man stands facing an immense stretch of darkness to the northeast.

Cancer 15°

A group of people who have eaten far past fullness, and enjoyed every bite of it.

Cancer 16°

A man sits before a square shape, an old handwritten scroll unrolled in front of him.

Cancer 17°

A single seed pushes upward, becoming, in time, both knowledge and life itself.

Cancer 18°

A hen scratches at the ground, working to find food for the chicks behind her.

Cancer 19°

A priest conducts a wedding, joining two people in front of witnesses.

Cancer 20°

Gondoliers sing to their passengers as the boats move through the evening canals.

Cancer 21°

A celebrated singer performs alone before a full, attentive audience.

Cancer 22°

A woman stands at the water's edge, watching for the sailboat that is coming.

Cancer 23°

Members of a literary society gather for their regular meeting.

Cancer 24°

A woman and two men, stranded together on a small island, share the same sunlit patch of land.

Cancer 25°

A sudden dark shadow, like a heavy cloak, falls across a man's right shoulder.

Cancer 26°

In a wealthy home's library, guests sit among the cushions, reading.

Cancer 27°

A fierce storm tears through a canyon lined with costly, secluded homes.

Cancer 28°

A Native woman, educated away from her tribe, returns and presents her partner to her people.

Cancer 29°

A Greek muse holds newborn twins on a golden scale, weighing them.

Cancer 30°

A woman descended from patriots walks proudly to the podium at her society's meeting.
