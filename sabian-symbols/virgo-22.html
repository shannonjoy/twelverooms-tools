THE SYMBOL

“A royal coat of arms hangs on display, its surface set with precious stones.”

Virgo 22° · Sabian symbols, devised 1925 (Marc Edmund Jones, clairvoyant Elsie Wheeler)

Remember to round up: a planet or point at 9°30′ takes the 10° symbol, not the 9° one.

What this degree describes

Ordinary heraldry gets elevated here past mere symbol into something genuinely valuable in its own right, meaning and material worth fused into a single object. This degree suggests that service, done at a high enough level for long enough, eventually earns exactly this kind of recognition: not just an emblem, but one worth something on its own terms. Virgo's humility about its own work meets, in this degree, the moment that work has actually accumulated enough weight to be honored openly.

When a planet or point falls here

A planet at this degree often shows recognition or status earned through long, careful service rather than claimed prematurely, worth that has genuinely accumulated rather than only been announced. Jupiter or Venus here can indicate real, well-deserved honor arriving eventually. On the Midheaven, it favors a reputation built slowly enough that, once achieved, it is entirely secure.

What careful, long service of yours is finally worth putting some actual jewels on, instead of downplaying it any further?
