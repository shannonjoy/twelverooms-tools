THE SYMBOL

“A professor lowers his glasses to look directly, closely, at the students in front of him.”

Libra 11° · Sabian symbols, devised 1925 (Marc Edmund Jones, clairvoyant Elsie Wheeler)

Remember to round up: a planet or point at 9°30′ takes the 10° symbol, not the 9° one.

What this degree describes

A small, exact gesture, glasses lowered just enough to see clearly, carries a whole relationship of authority and attention in one motion. This degree is Libra's discernment made almost precise: not a lecture, just a look that says the professor is actually paying attention to who's in the room and how they're doing. Judgment, here, is careful and completely without theater.

When a planet or point falls here

A planet at this degree often shows real discernment expressed through close, individual attention rather than broad instruction, someone who notices exactly who needs what. Mercury or Saturn here can favor a teacher, mentor, or evaluator whose judgment is trusted because it is so specific. On the sixth house, it can describe expertise applied one careful case at a time.

Who is currently getting your full, close attention, over the glasses, rather than your general one?
