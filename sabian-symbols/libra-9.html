THE SYMBOL

“Three works by old master painters hang together in their own dedicated room of a gallery.”

Libra 9° · Sabian symbols, devised 1925 (Marc Edmund Jones, clairvoyant Elsie Wheeler)

Remember to round up: a planet or point at 9°30′ takes the 10° symbol, not the 9° one.

What this degree describes

Excellence, hung together on purpose, so it can be studied side by side instead of admired one canvas at a time. This degree is Libra's aesthetic judgment at its most disciplined: not just liking beautiful things, but curating them, deciding which three belong in the same room because together they teach something none of them could alone. Comparison, done with real taste, becomes its own form of insight.

When a planet or point falls here

A planet at this degree often shows a refined, comparative eye, the ability to place excellent things next to each other and see exactly what each one is doing that the others aren't. Venus here favors real connoisseurship, judgment trained rather than merely inherited. On the fifth house, it can describe creative work built by studying masters closely enough to know precisely what to take from each.

Which three influences would you actually hang together in your own private gallery, and what would they teach each other?
