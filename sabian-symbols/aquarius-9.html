Aquarius 9°

The traditional symbol for this degree, one of the 360 Sabian symbols first recorded in 1925: “A flag, caught by the wind, seems to transform into a rising eagle.”

THE SYMBOL

“A flag, caught by the wind, seems to transform into a rising eagle.”

Aquarius 9° · Sabian symbols, devised 1925 (Marc Edmund Jones, clairvoyant Elsie Wheeler)

Remember to round up: a planet or point at 9°30′ takes the 10° symbol, not the 9° one.

What this degree describes

Cloth becomes creature: what was only a symbol, sewn and hung to represent an idea, suddenly appears to take on the living force of the thing it stood for. This degree is about conviction made kinetic, an ideal that stops being decorative and starts to act like it actually believes in itself. Aquarius cares about ideals more than almost any other sign, and this degree shows what happens when an ideal finally has enough wind under it to leave the pole.

When a planet or point falls here

A planet at this degree often shows an ideal or cause that has genuinely come alive in someone's actions, not just their stated beliefs. Jupiter or Uranus here can indicate a real capacity to make a symbol functional, to turn stated values into visible motion. It rewards checking whether your own flag is still just fabric, or whether it has actually started to fly on its own.

Which of your stated ideals has actually grown wings, and which one is still just cloth on a pole?
