How to use this tool

Enter your birth details above (city or coordinates, date, and time if you know it), then pick any date in the calendar. Don't know your birth time? Leave it blank and you'll get an honest horoscope based on Sun, Moon, and planets—just without rising-sign specificity. The tool works better with an exact time, especially if your Ascendant or Midheaven are key to the day's energy.

Common questions

What's a transit?

A transit is when a planet moving through the sky makes an angle to a planet in your natal chart. It's the conversation between the living sky and your birth imprint. Some transits bring ease; others bring friction. All of them are opportunities for growth.

Why are some times exact and others say "in orb"?

The Moon and inner planets (Sun, Mercury, Venus, Mars) move fast enough to perfect an aspect to the exact minute. Slower planets (Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune, Pluto) hold an aspect for days or weeks without a precise moment—we show when they're active rather than a fake time.

Can I see horoscopes for past dates?

Yes. Pick any date in the calendar, past or future. The horoscope is computed fresh from the actual sky on that date, not from templates or memory.

How is this different from a free sun-sign horoscope?

This is personal to your full chart: your Sun, Moon, Ascendant, and every other placement. It reads your specific natal points against the day's transits, not a generic "Virgo" forecast that treats 1 billion people the same.
