Pisces 28°

The traditional symbol for this degree, one of the 360 Sabian symbols first recorded in 1925: “Under a full moon, a fertile garden shows off a wide variety of ripened vegetables.”

THE SYMBOL

“Under a full moon, a fertile garden shows off a wide variety of ripened vegetables.”

Pisces 28° · Sabian symbols, devised 1925 (Marc Edmund Jones, clairvoyant Elsie Wheeler)

Remember to round up: a planet or point at 9°30′ takes the 10° symbol, not the 9° one.

What this degree describes

Abundance, this time literally underfoot rather than overhead: the moon simply provides enough light to actually see what the garden has already produced, row after row of different, fully grown food. This degree trusts that plenty, once it has been properly tended, does not need daylight to prove itself, moonlight is more than enough to reveal how much is actually there.

When a planet or point falls here

A planet at this degree often shows real, tangible abundance produced through patient, sustained tending rather than sudden windfall. Venus or Jupiter here can indicate someone whose garden, of whatever kind, has genuinely come in full. It rewards actually walking the rows at night to see everything that has quietly ripened.

What have you been patiently tending that has already, quietly, come in full, whether or not you have walked out to actually look at it yet?
