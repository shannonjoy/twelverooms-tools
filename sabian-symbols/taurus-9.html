Taurus 9°

The traditional symbol for this degree, one of the 360 Sabian symbols first recorded in 1925: “A fully decorated Christmas tree.”

THE SYMBOL

“A fully decorated Christmas tree.”

Taurus 9° · Sabian symbols, devised 1925 (Marc Edmund Jones, clairvoyant Elsie Wheeler)

Remember to round up: a planet or point at 9°30′ takes the 10° symbol, not the 9° one.

What this degree describes

Abundance, deliberately assembled: nothing on this tree arrived by accident, every ornament placed by someone who wanted the whole effect to be complete. This degree is Taurus at its most openly festive, taking real pleasure in accumulation done well, in a display that exists purely to be beautiful and shared. There's craft in it too, not just quantity, a fully decorated tree is the result of care, layer by layer, until abundance itself becomes the art. Where degree four chased treasure that kept receding, this one simply has the treasure, hung and lit and finished.

When a planet or point falls here

A planet at this degree often shows a genuine gift for gathering and arranging abundance so it can be enjoyed by everyone in the room, not hoarded privately. Venus here favors real skill at making a shared space feel rich and celebratory. It rewards trusting that some abundance exists purely to be displayed and given away with pleasure.

What have you finished decorating, fully, that you haven't yet let anyone else come see?
