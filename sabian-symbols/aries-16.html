Aries 16°

The traditional symbol for this degree, one of the 360 Sabian symbols first recorded in 1925: “Nature spirits dance in the light of the setting sun.”

THE SYMBOL

“Nature spirits dance in the light of the setting sun.”

Aries 16° · Sabian symbols, devised 1925 (Marc Edmund Jones, clairvoyant Elsie Wheeler)

Remember to round up: a planet or point at 9°30′ takes the 10° symbol, not the 9° one.

What this degree describes

A threshold hour, and creatures that only really show themselves in it: not full daylight, not night, the brief gold window when the ordinary rules loosen a little. This degree favors the liminal, the almost-unseen layer of life that operates just past what practical daytime attention usually notices. Aries meets its own wilder, less literal edge here, the part of instinct that borders on enchantment rather than mere drive.

When a planet or point falls here

A planet at this degree often brings sensitivity to atmosphere, timing, and the parts of a moment that don't show up on a schedule. Neptune or the Moon here can describe someone genuinely attuned to twilight states, transitions, the in-between of any process. It rewards paying attention right at the edges of things, not only their broad daylight middle.
