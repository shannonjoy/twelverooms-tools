Aquarius 14°

The traditional symbol for this degree, one of the 360 Sabian symbols first recorded in 1925: “A train enters a tunnel, and its interior view goes dark.”

THE SYMBOL

“A train enters a tunnel, and its interior view goes dark.”

Aquarius 14° · Sabian symbols, devised 1925 (Marc Edmund Jones, clairvoyant Elsie Wheeler)

Remember to round up: a planet or point at 9°30′ takes the 10° symbol, not the 9° one.

What this degree describes

Momentum continues, but visibility disappears entirely: this is a stretch of the journey that has to be traveled by trust rather than sight. Aquarius, oriented so strongly toward what's ahead, meets here a passage where the future is briefly unreadable, and the only real choice is to keep moving at the same speed rather than stop inside the dark. The tunnel has an end; the discomfort is temporary, but it's real while it lasts, and this degree doesn't pretend otherwise.

When a planet or point falls here

A planet at this degree often shows a capacity to keep functioning through a stretch of genuine uncertainty, trusting the track even when nothing outside the window confirms it. Pluto or Saturn here can indicate real resilience through opaque, disorienting transitions. It rewards staying on schedule through the dark part rather than assuming the tunnel means the journey has failed.
