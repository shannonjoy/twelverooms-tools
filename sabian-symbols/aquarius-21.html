Aquarius 21°

The traditional symbol for this degree, one of the 360 Sabian symbols first recorded in 1925: “A woman, disappointed and disillusioned, faces a life that suddenly looks empty, and does so with real courage.”

THE SYMBOL

“A woman, disappointed and disillusioned, faces a life that suddenly looks empty, and does so with real courage.”

Aquarius 21° · Sabian symbols, devised 1925 (Marc Edmund Jones, clairvoyant Elsie Wheeler)

Remember to round up: a planet or point at 9°30′ takes the 10° symbol, not the 9° one.

What this degree describes

The hope that organized her expectations has collapsed, and what's left is bare: no comforting illusion left standing, just the actual shape of her circumstances. This degree doesn't rush to console; it sits with real disappointment and asks what courage looks like once the fantasy is gone. Aquarius's idealism runs high, and this degree shows what happens when the ideal doesn't arrive, and the sign has to find out whether its commitment to the future can survive contact with genuine loss.

When a planet or point falls here

A planet at this degree often shows someone who has faced real disillusionment and kept standing anyway, courage built specifically from disappointment rather than in spite of it. Saturn or Chiron here can describe a hard-won, unsentimental resilience. It rewards honoring what was actually lost, rather than skipping straight to forced optimism.

What illusion have you already had to let go of, and what does your courage actually look like now that it's gone?
