Capricorn 25°

The traditional symbol for this degree, one of the 360 Sabian symbols first recorded in 1925: “A shop stands full, floor to ceiling, with fine, richly patterned rugs.”

THE SYMBOL

“A shop stands full, floor to ceiling, with fine, richly patterned rugs.”

Capricorn 25° · Sabian symbols, devised 1925 (Marc Edmund Jones, clairvoyant Elsie Wheeler)

Remember to round up: a planet or point at 9°30′ takes the 10° symbol, not the 9° one.

What this degree describes

Value, here, has been gathered patiently from far away and laid out for anyone with the eye to actually recognize it. Capricorn's relationship to material wealth shows itself plainly in this degree: not flashy accumulation, but real, discerning collection, each rug chosen for genuine craft rather than mere volume. There is expertise required simply to run a shop like this well, knowing what is actually fine from what only looks fine.

When a planet or point falls here

A planet at this degree often shows real discernment around material value, an eye trained well enough to tell genuine craft from mere appearance. Venus or Saturn here can indicate a gift for accumulating quality deliberately, over real time, rather than quickly or carelessly. It rewards trusting your own trained eye over the easier, louder claims of a room.

What have you collected slowly enough, and with a good enough eye, that its actual value is finally starting to show?
