Scorpio 24°

The traditional symbol for this degree, one of the 360 Sabian symbols first recorded in 1925: “Crowds come down from the mountain to hear a single man speak.”

THE SYMBOL

“Crowds come down from the mountain to hear a single man speak.”

Scorpio 24° · Sabian symbols, devised 1925 (Marc Edmund Jones, clairvoyant Elsie Wheeler)

Remember to round up: a planet or point at 9°30′ takes the 10° symbol, not the 9° one.

What this degree describes

Many people, drawn by one voice, willing to leave higher, safer ground and gather to listen. This degree is about real magnetism, not manufactured, since no one in the crowd was forced down the slope, but a pull genuine enough that people chose the descent for the sake of hearing what one person actually had to say.

When a planet or point falls here

A planet here often shows real, earned magnetism, the capacity to draw people toward a single message or presence without needing to force the gathering. Jupiter or the Sun can indicate someone whose voice is worth an actual crowd making the trip down to hear.

What have you had to say that would genuinely be worth someone else's whole descent from the mountain to come hear?
