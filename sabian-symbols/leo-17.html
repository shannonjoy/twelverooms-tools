Leo 17°

The traditional symbol for this degree, one of the 360 Sabian symbols first recorded in 1925: “A choir of ordinary volunteers, without robes, sings hymns together.”

THE SYMBOL

“A choir of ordinary volunteers, without robes, sings hymns together.”

Leo 17° · Sabian symbols, devised 1925 (Marc Edmund Jones, clairvoyant Elsie Wheeler)

Remember to round up: a planet or point at 9°30′ takes the 10° symbol, not the 9° one.

What this degree describes

Devotion, offered without the usual costume: this choir sings the same hymns a robed choir would, but stripped of the formal uniform, the singing itself carries the whole weight of the offering. Leo, so associated with performance, meets here a version of expression that needs no polish or vestment to be completely sincere. The music is real whether or not the choir looks the part.

When a planet or point falls here

A planet at this degree often shows heartfelt expression that does not depend on formal presentation to be genuine. Venus or Neptune here can indicate real, unpretentious devotion expressed through voice or craft. It rewards trusting that sincerity outperforms costume.

What are you currently offering wholeheartedly, even without the formal trappings that usually go with it?
