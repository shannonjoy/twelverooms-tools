Gemini 1°

The traditional symbol for this degree, one of the 360 Sabian symbols first recorded in 1925: “A boat with a glass bottom lets its passengers watch the underwater world glide by beneath them.”

THE SYMBOL

“A boat with a glass bottom lets its passengers watch the underwater world glide by beneath them.”

Gemini 1° · Sabian symbols, devised 1925 (Marc Edmund Jones, clairvoyant Elsie Wheeler)

Remember to round up: a planet or point at 9°30′ takes the 10° symbol, not the 9° one.

What this degree describes

This is Gemini's opening move, announced in a single image: stay on the surface, and still see clearly all the way down. The boat is the trick, a vessel built specifically so curiosity never has to get wet to be satisfied. Nothing about this degree asks you to dive in; it asks you to build something that lets you look without needing to submerge. Two levels of reality, held in view at the very same time, is the whole sign in miniature, right at its first degree.

When a planet or point falls here

A planet here often grants a gift for seeing beneath the surface of a situation without disturbing it, a natural researcher or observer. Mercury at this degree favors a mind built to translate between what is visible and what is quietly happening underneath it. On an angle, it can describe someone the world experiences as already seeing what is really going on.

What is gliding along beneath you right now that you've only ever watched, never actually touched?
