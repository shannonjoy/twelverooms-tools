THE SYMBOL

“Sunrise breaks over a landscape, and every single thing in it looks different than it did the night before.”

Libra 3° · Sabian symbols, devised 1925 (Marc Edmund Jones, clairvoyant Elsie Wheeler)

Remember to round up: a planet or point at 9°30′ takes the 10° symbol, not the 9° one.

What this degree describes

Morning arrives, and the whole visible world is suddenly a different one, not because anything was rebuilt overnight but because the light itself changed what can be seen. This degree trusts that transformation doesn't always require effort, sometimes it only requires the sun coming up on a situation that was already different and simply hadn't been looked at properly yet. Libra, forever weighing what it perceives, gets here the reminder that perception itself can be the whole revolution.

When a planet or point falls here

A planet at this degree often marks sudden clarity or a shift in outlook that makes a situation genuinely legible in a way it wasn't the night before. The Sun or Uranus here can indicate real capacity for abrupt, total reframes rather than slow revision. On the Ascendant, it can describe someone whose whole presentation seems to reset overnight, more than once in a life.

What already changed, that you're only now, this morning, actually able to see?
