THE SYMBOL

“An old flag is lowered from a ship's mast, and a new one raised in its place.”

Cancer 1° · Sabian symbols, devised 1925 (Marc Edmund Jones, clairvoyant Elsie Wheeler)

Remember to round up: a planet or point at 9°30′ takes the 10° symbol, not the 9° one.

What this degree describes

Cancer opens with a formal changing of the watch rather than a first breath: the vessel does not change, only what flies from it changes, a signal to everyone watching that authority, mood, or belonging has shifted hands. This is the sign's first lesson, that home is not a fixed banner but one you keep replacing as you become someone new, while the actual ship of self sails on unbroken underneath. There is ceremony in it, not carelessness, since a flag lowered gets folded before the next one goes up.

When a planet or point falls here

A planet at Cancer 1 often marks a deliberate turning point in how someone signals belonging, family name, home base, primary loyalty, changed on purpose rather than drifted into. The Moon here can show someone who consciously chooses, more than once, what home means to her. On an angle, it suggests a person the world reads through whichever flag is currently flying, aware a past one still sits folded and kept.

What flag are you currently flying, and did you fold the last one with care before you raised it?
