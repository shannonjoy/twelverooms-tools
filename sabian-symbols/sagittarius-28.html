THE SYMBOL

“An old stone bridge, built long ago, still carries travelers across a beautiful stream every day.”

Sagittarius 28° · Sabian symbols, devised 1925 (Marc Edmund Jones, clairvoyant Elsie Wheeler)

Remember to round up: a planet or point at 9°30′ takes the 10° symbol, not the 9° one.

What this degree describes

Some crossings get built once and simply keep working, structure so sound that generations later, people are still relying on it without giving its age a second thought. Sagittarius is drawn to new horizons, but this degree honors the old, well-built passage that makes crossing to any new horizon possible in the first place. There is real beauty in the stream underneath too, a reminder that the bridge was built to serve something worth crossing toward, not just to span an obstacle.

When a planet or point falls here

A planet at this degree often shows real reliance on something old and well-built, a belief, a relationship, a piece of infrastructure, that continues to work exactly as intended long after it was made. Saturn here is especially at home, favoring durable structures over flashy new ones. It rewards trusting the old bridge instead of always looking for a newer crossing.

What old, sturdy crossing in your life are you still relying on every day, without ever quite thanking it?
