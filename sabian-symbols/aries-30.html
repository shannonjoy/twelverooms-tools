THE SYMBOL

“A duck pond, with its brood.”

Aries 30° · Sabian symbols, devised 1925 (Marc Edmund Jones, clairvoyant Elsie Wheeler)

What this degree describes

The final degree of Aries, and the sign's whole restless momentum settles, for a moment, into something small, contained, and quietly thriving. A pond is not the ocean it started from in degree one; it is modest, bounded, and entirely sufficient for the life it holds. The brood, newly hatched, is proof that everything this sign spent thirty degrees becoming has actually produced something: not a grand finale, just a calm, complete little scene of new life doing fine on its own pond.

When a planet or point falls here

A planet at this degree often shows contentment achieved in a modest, self-contained sphere rather than a sprawling one, and a genuine ease raising or tending what has newly come into being. The Moon here is especially at home: nurturing that doesn't need a bigger stage to feel finished. It rewards recognizing when “enough” has actually already arrived.

What small pond have you built that is already, quietly, enough?
