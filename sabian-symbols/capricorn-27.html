The traditional symbol for this degree, one of the 360 Sabian symbols first recorded in 1925: “Pilgrims climb a long, steep stairway toward a shrine set high on a mountain.”

THE SYMBOL

“Pilgrims climb a long, steep stairway toward a shrine set high on a mountain.”

Capricorn 27° · Sabian symbols, devised 1925 (Marc Edmund Jones, clairvoyant Elsie Wheeler)

Remember to round up: a planet or point at 9°30′ takes the 10° symbol, not the 9° one.

What this degree describes

The climb itself is the discipline here, each worn step a small act of devotion repeated by countless travelers before this one. Capricorn's whole nature is contained in this image, the mountain as destination and as method both, arrival meaning nothing without the deliberate, difficult ascent that earns it. This degree honors effort undertaken specifically because the goal is sacred, not merely because the goal is high.

When a planet or point falls here

A planet at this degree often shows real willingness to undertake a long, demanding ascent toward something genuinely meaningful, discipline in service of devotion rather than mere achievement. Saturn or Jupiter here can indicate someone for whom the difficulty of the climb is inseparable from the value of the destination. It rewards trusting that the steps themselves are part of what you came for.
