Taurus 27°

The traditional symbol for this degree, one of the 360 Sabian symbols first recorded in 1925: “An elderly woman sells handmade beads at a roadside stand.”

THE SYMBOL

“An elderly woman sells handmade beads at a roadside stand.”

Taurus 27° · Sabian symbols, devised 1925 (Marc Edmund Jones, clairvoyant Elsie Wheeler)

Remember to round up: a planet or point at 9°30′ takes the 10° symbol, not the 9° one.

Note: the 1925 original for this degree used dated ethnic imagery; the picture above keeps its meaning in respectful, modern terms.

What this degree describes

Small commerce, carried out by someone with decades of practice at exactly this trade, offering handmade, meaningful goods for whatever a passerby can pay. This degree honors craft that has been kept alive across a long life and possibly a long lineage, dignity intact even when the setting is modest and the stakes are small. Taurus values what is made by hand and what endures, and this degree shows both at once, an elder still doing the work, still trading it fairly.

When a planet or point falls here

A planet at this degree often shows real respect for small-scale, handmade commerce and the long experience behind it, valuing the maker as much as the object made. Saturn or Venus here can indicate someone whose worth was built slowly, trade by trade, over a very long time. It rewards paying full price for what took a lifetime to learn how to make.

What have you been quietly making and trading for a long time, that deserves to be valued at its real worth?
