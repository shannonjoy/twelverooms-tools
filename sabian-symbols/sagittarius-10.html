Sagittarius 10°

The traditional symbol for this degree, one of the 360 Sabian symbols first recorded in 1925: “On a stage, an actress plays a golden-haired goddess of opportunity, offering her gifts to whoever steps forward.”

THE SYMBOL

“On a stage, an actress plays a golden-haired goddess of opportunity, offering her gifts to whoever steps forward.”

Sagittarius 10° · Sabian symbols, devised 1925 (Marc Edmund Jones, clairvoyant Elsie Wheeler)

Remember to round up: a planet or point at 9°30′ takes the 10° symbol, not the 9° one.

What this degree describes

Opportunity, dramatized: this degree admits that luck often needs a little theater to actually be recognized and seized, a costume and a spotlight helping people believe the chance in front of them is real. Sagittarius's famous good fortune is not passive here, it is staged, presented, made vivid enough that someone actually has the nerve to walk up and take it. The goddess is played by an actress, not a deity, a reminder that opportunity usually needs a human being willing to perform the part of stepping forward.

When a planet or point falls here

A planet at this degree often shows real talent for recognizing and dramatizing a chance so that others, or the self, can actually see and take it. Jupiter or Venus here can indicate a gift for making opportunity feel vivid and available rather than abstract. It rewards playing your own part in the moment instead of waiting for the goddess to come find you off-stage.
