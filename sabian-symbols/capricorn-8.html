Sabian Symbols · Capricorn · Degree 8 of 30

Capricorn 8°

The traditional symbol for this degree, one of the 360 Sabian symbols first recorded in 1925: “In a sunlit house, tame birds sing without any apparent reason to hold back.”

THE SYMBOL

“In a sunlit house, tame birds sing without any apparent reason to hold back.”

Capricorn 8° · Sabian symbols, devised 1925 (Marc Edmund Jones, clairvoyant Elsie Wheeler)

Remember to round up: a planet or point at 9°30′ takes the 10° symbol, not the 9° one.

What this degree describes

Nothing here is performing for an audience; the birds sing because the house is warm and they are safe in it, joy with no occasion required beyond the ordinary comfort of home. Capricorn, so associated with effort and climb, gets a rare picture of achievement already arrived, contentment that does not need to be earned again today. This is the reward sitting quietly inside the domestic structure the sign spends so much of its energy building.

When a planet or point falls here

A planet at this degree often shows real, uncomplicated contentment inside a well-built domestic life, joy that does not require an external occasion to justify itself. Venus or the Moon here can indicate a home genuinely warm enough that everyone in it, without prompting, is glad to be there. On the fourth house, it favors a household whose comfort was actually built, not simply inherited by luck.

What in your own house is singing right now, simply because the conditions you built for it are finally good enough?
