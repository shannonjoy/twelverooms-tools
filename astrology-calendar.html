Common questions

How do I add the astrology calendar to Google Calendar?

On a computer, open Google Calendar, choose the plus sign next to Other calendars, pick From URL, and paste https://thetwelverooms.com/calendar/twelve-rooms.ics. Or use the Add to Google Calendar button on this page. It then appears on your phone too.

How do I add it to Apple Calendar on iPhone?

Tap Subscribe on Apple Calendar on this page and confirm. Or go to Settings, Calendar, Accounts, Add Account, Other, Add Subscribed Calendar, and enter https://thetwelverooms.com/calendar/twelve-rooms.ics.

What is in the calendar?

For 2027: 12 Full Moons with their almanac names, 13 New Moons, 5 eclipses, and the Mercury and Mars retrogrades as all-day spans, each with its exact time, sign and degree.

Does it update itself?

Yes, if you subscribe rather than download. Subscribed calendars refresh on their own, so new dates appear as the feed is extended. A downloaded file is a one-time copy.

Is it free?

Yes. No account, no sign-up.
