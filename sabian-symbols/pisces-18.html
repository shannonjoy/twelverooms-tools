Sabian Symbols · Pisces · Degree 18 of 30

Pisces 18°

The traditional symbol for this degree, one of the 360 Sabian symbols first recorded in 1925: “Inside an enormous tent, a well-known preacher holds a crowd with a dramatic revival meeting.”

THE SYMBOL

“Inside an enormous tent, a well-known preacher holds a crowd with a dramatic revival meeting.”

Pisces 18° · Sabian symbols, devised 1925 (Marc Edmund Jones, clairvoyant Elsie Wheeler)

Remember to round up: a planet or point at 9°30′ takes the 10° symbol, not the 9° one.

What this degree describes

Faith, staged for maximum effect, and genuinely moving people anyway: the tent is huge on purpose, the performance is deliberate, and the emotion in the crowd is nonetheless real. This degree asks Pisces to hold two truths that usually feel opposed, that showmanship and sincerity can occupy the exact same tent, that a crowd swept up by spectacle can still be having a real spiritual experience underneath the theater of it.

When a planet or point falls here

A planet at this degree often shows genuine charisma capable of moving a large group emotionally, sincerity and performance working together rather than against each other. Neptune or the Sun here can indicate real gifts for public, large-scale inspiration. It rewards checking, honestly, whether the feeling being stirred is actually true, not just theatrical.

Where in your own life are real feeling and real performance currently happening at the exact same time, and are you sure which one is leading?
