THE SYMBOL

“A soul waits quietly for its chance to be fully expressed in the world.”

Leo 14° · Sabian symbols, devised 1925 (Marc Edmund Jones, clairvoyant Elsie Wheeler)

Remember to round up: a planet or point at 9°30′ takes the 10° symbol, not the 9° one.

What this degree describes

Not yet visible, but entirely ready: this degree holds a self that exists in full already, waiting only for its chance to be shown to the world. Leo's whole project is exactly this, an inner brightness insisting on outward form, and this degree names the waiting room before that happens, the real readiness that precedes the debut. Nothing is missing here except opportunity; the substance is already whole.

When a planet or point falls here

A planet at this degree often shows genuine inner readiness ahead of outer opportunity, a self that is complete before circumstance catches up to let it show. The Sun here can indicate someone whose real self is already formed and simply waiting for its stage. It rewards trusting the readiness even while the stage is still being built.

What in you is already fully formed, just waiting for the room that will finally let it show?
