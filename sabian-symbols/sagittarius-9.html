THE SYMBOL

“A mother leads her small children step by step up a steep staircase.”

Sagittarius 9° · Sabian symbols, devised 1925 (Marc Edmund Jones, clairvoyant Elsie Wheeler)

Remember to round up: a planet or point at 9°30′ takes the 10° symbol, not the 9° one.

What this degree describes

Ascent, here, is a shared project, patient and deliberate, one small hand held at a time. This degree grounds Sagittarius's love of climbing toward something higher in the most ordinary possible scene, a parent making sure nobody gets left behind on a genuinely difficult flight of stairs. The height at the top matters less, for now, than the steady, careful pace it actually takes to get everyone there together.

When a planet or point falls here

A planet at this degree often shows real care taken to bring others along on a difficult climb, rather than simply reaching the top alone. The Moon or Saturn here can indicate a natural teacher or guide, someone who paces a hard ascent to the slowest person's steps. It rewards measuring your climb by who made it up with you.
