How these dates are chosen

Publishing is Mercury (the written word) lifted by Jupiter (a wide audience) and the Sun (recognition). The Moon should be waxing, not void of course, and applying to one of these, away from Mars and Saturn. Above all, Mercury direct, so the words land as written.

What to avoid

Mercury retrograde is the one to dodge for anything built on words: Feb 9 to Mar 3, Jun 10 to Jul 4, Oct 7 to 28. The eclipse seasons around Feb 6 and 20 and around Jul 18, Aug 2, and Aug 17 are loud, unpredictable ground for a debut you want read on its merits.

Common questions

What is the best day to publish a book in 2027?

2027's strongest days to publish a book, on the Twelve Rooms Timing Scale: January 13, April 23, and July 12 (Good). The strongest days fall on a waxing Moon applying to Mercury, Jupiter, or the Sun, with Mercury direct. The table above gives the best such day in each month of 2027.

Why keep Mercury direct for publishing?

Mercury rules writing, print, and the message itself. Releasing under a retrograde Mercury (Feb 9 to Mar 3, Jun 10 to Jul 4, Oct 7 to 28 in 2027) invites mix-ups and revisions, so the dates keep it direct.

How are these dates calculated?

By Western electional astrology, computed with the Swiss Ephemeris in whole-sign houses. The exact best hour depends on your location, since the angles and planetary hours are local.
