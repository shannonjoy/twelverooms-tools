Aquarius 28°

The traditional symbol for this degree, one of the 360 Sabian symbols first recorded in 1925: “A tree is cut down and sawed into logs, stored up for winter's supply.”

THE SYMBOL

“A tree is cut down and sawed into logs, stored up for winter's supply.”

Aquarius 28° · Sabian symbols, devised 1925 (Marc Edmund Jones, clairvoyant Elsie Wheeler)

Remember to round up: a planet or point at 9°30′ takes the 10° symbol, not the 9° one.

What this degree describes

Something living is deliberately ended so that warmth and fuel exist later, when the season turns hard. This degree doesn't flinch from the cost: the tree is genuinely gone, and the sawing is real labor, but the winter ahead is real too, and this is how a household actually survives it. Aquarius plans for the collective future, and this degree shows the sign willing to make a hard, concrete choice now so that a whole household stays warm later.

When a planet or point falls here

A planet at this degree often shows a willingness to make a difficult, even costly choice now specifically to secure resources for a harder season ahead. Saturn here is especially at home, favoring practical foresight over sentimentality when provision is genuinely at stake. It rewards making the cut before winter arrives, not scrambling for wood once it has.

What have you already had to fell and cut, so that you'd actually have enough stored up for what's coming?
