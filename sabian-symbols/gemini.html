Gemini: all 30 Sabian symbols

Every whole degree of Gemini has its own Sabian symbol, a small traditional picture first recorded in 1925. Find the exact degree of your Sun, Moon, Ascendant, or any planet in Gemini, and read what that degree adds to it.

Gemini 1°

A boat with a glass bottom lets its passengers watch the underwater world glide by beneath them.

Gemini 2°

A cloaked figure moves from house to house at night, filling stockings while everyone sleeps.

Gemini 3°

An old engraving shows elegant strollers moving through a formal palace garden, dressed in the fashion of a vanished era.

Gemini 4°

Sprigs of holly and mistletoe, hung again this year, bring back every Christmas that came before this one.

Gemini 5°

A newly printed pamphlet argues its case and calls its readers to act right now.

Gemini 6°

A crew works a rig, boring steadily down through rock toward a reserve of oil they are certain is there.

Gemini 7°

A rope and bucket wait at an old well, resting in the cool shade of tall, long-established trees.

Gemini 8°

Workers gather outside the factory gates, worked up and refusing to go back in until conditions change.

Gemini 9°

A quiver, packed full and ready, holds every arrow that has not yet been shot.

Gemini 10°

A small plane tips forward and drops fast, nose first, toward the ground.

Gemini 11°

Land that was closed off yesterday stands open today, and the first ones through are already imagining what to build there.

Gemini 12°

A young woman held as property speaks up sharply to the one who claims to own her, and will not be quieted.

Gemini 13°

A gifted musician sits at the keyboard, playing with the whole of their attention.

Gemini 14°

Two people, at a real distance from each other, understand each other without needing to say a word.

Gemini 15°

Two children compare notes in their own language, teaching each other whatever each one already knows.

Gemini 16°

A woman stands before a crowd, making her case with real feeling, determined that her cause be heard.

Gemini 17°

A strong young face slowly settles into the more thoughtful, weathered face of someone who has learned to think things through.

Gemini 18°

Two men speak their own language together, at home in it, in a city built around a completely different one.

Gemini 19°

A huge, ancient book lies open, holding wisdom far older than anyone currently reading it.

Gemini 20°

Long tables offer dish after dish, gathered in from many different places, more choice than anyone could eat.

Gemini 21°

A crowd of workers marches together, loud and unified, demanding to be heard.

Gemini 22°

Couples fill an old barn, turning the space that stored the harvest into a floor for dancing.

Gemini 23°

Three young birds sit crowded together in a nest built high up in a tree.

Gemini 24°

Children glide across the ice covering a small village pond, laughing as they go.

Gemini 25°

A gardener works carefully through a row of tall palms, trimming each one back into shape.

Gemini 26°

Frost coats every branch in the woods overnight, and the whole forest briefly looks made of glass.

Gemini 27°

A wanderer steps out from the trees and stands looking, wide-eyed, at the open country ahead.

Gemini 28°

A man walks out of the courthouse having lost everything financially, feeling both wrecked and oddly ready to begin again.

Gemini 29°

High in a still-bare tree, a mockingbird runs through its whole range of songs before the season has properly turned.

Gemini 30°

A line of women in swimwear parades past the judges and the crowd gathered at the shore.
