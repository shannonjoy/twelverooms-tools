Sagittarius 17°

The traditional symbol for this degree, one of the 360 Sabian symbols first recorded in 1925: “A crowd gathers on a hillside at dawn for an Easter sunrise service.”

THE SYMBOL

“A crowd gathers on a hillside at dawn for an Easter sunrise service.”

Sagittarius 17° · Sabian symbols, devised 1925 (Marc Edmund Jones, clairvoyant Elsie Wheeler)

Remember to round up: a planet or point at 9°30′ takes the 10° symbol, not the 9° one.

What this degree describes

Faith, here, gets up early and climbs to a real vantage point to meet the light directly, rather than waiting for a more convenient hour indoors. This degree is Sagittarius's spiritual appetite at its most communal and most literal, a whole crowd choosing to be present for the exact moment belief and daybreak arrive together. There is real effort in the gathering, the climb, the early hour, that makes the eventual light feel earned rather than simply given.

When a planet or point falls here

A planet at this degree often shows genuine devotion expressed through shared ritual and real physical effort, not just private belief held quietly. Jupiter or the Sun here can indicate someone who draws real meaning from being present, in a crowd, for a significant threshold moment. It rewards showing up early for what actually matters to you.
