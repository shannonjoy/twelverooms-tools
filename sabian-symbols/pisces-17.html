Pisces 17°

The traditional symbol for this degree, one of the 360 Sabian symbols first recorded in 1925: “A crowd walks an Easter promenade, dressed for the occasion of new life.”

THE SYMBOL

“A crowd walks an Easter promenade, dressed for the occasion of new life.”

Pisces 17° · Sabian symbols, devised 1925 (Marc Edmund Jones, clairvoyant Elsie Wheeler)

Remember to round up: a planet or point at 9°30′ takes the 10° symbol, not the 9° one.

What this degree describes

Renewal, made public and communal: a whole street of people, dressed brightly, walking together specifically to mark the return of life after a long dormant season. This degree gives Pisces, so associated with endings, a genuine picture of what comes after one: not private relief, but a shared, visible celebration, everyone choosing to be seen at exactly the moment resurrection is being marked.

When a planet or point falls here

A planet at this degree often shows real capacity to celebrate renewal openly and communally rather than only privately. Venus or Jupiter here can indicate a genuine gift for marking new beginnings with real, visible joy. It rewards trusting that some resurrections are actually meant to be walked in public.
