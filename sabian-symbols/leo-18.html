THE SYMBOL

“A teacher runs a chemistry experiment live in front of a room of students.”

Leo 18° · Sabian symbols, devised 1925 (Marc Edmund Jones, clairvoyant Elsie Wheeler)

Remember to round up: a planet or point at 9°30′ takes the 10° symbol, not the 9° one.

What this degree describes

Knowledge, demonstrated live rather than just described: the teacher performs the experiment in front of the room specifically so the students can watch the reaction happen in real time. Leo's instinct to perform finds real purpose here, teaching as a kind of theater with an actual educational payoff, showmanship in service of genuine understanding rather than mere display.

When a planet or point falls here

A planet at this degree often shows a gift for teaching through demonstration, making an idea vivid and visible rather than only explained. Mercury or Jupiter here can indicate real talent for turning instruction into something the room actually watches, engaged. It rewards performing your knowledge, not just stating it.
