THE SYMBOL

“A path runs through woods lit up in full, brilliant autumn color.”

Scorpio 18° · Sabian symbols, devised 1925 (Marc Edmund Jones, clairvoyant Elsie Wheeler)

Remember to round up: a planet or point at 9°30′ takes the 10° symbol, not the 9° one.

What this degree describes

Beauty tied specifically to a season of dying back, the most vivid color arriving right before the leaves fall entirely. Scorpio understands this paradox intimately: that things often become most striking exactly at the point they are ending, that decline and radiance are not opposites here but the very same moment, seen from two different angles.

When a planet or point falls here

A planet here often shows beauty or intensity that peaks specifically during a transition or an ending. Neptune or Venus can indicate someone whose most vivid work, or most vivid feeling, arrives right at a closing chapter rather than at its beginning.
