Sabian Symbols · Leo · Degree 30 of 30

Leo 30°

The traditional symbol for this degree, one of the 360 Sabian symbols first recorded in 1925: “A letter full of private news is folded closed, its envelope left unsealed.”

THE SYMBOL

“A letter full of private news is folded closed, its envelope left unsealed.”

Leo 30° · Sabian symbols, devised 1925 (Marc Edmund Jones, clairvoyant Elsie Wheeler)

Remember to round up: a planet or point at 9°30′ takes the 10° symbol, not the 9° one.

What this degree describes

The final degree of Leo closes on something important, written and folded, but deliberately left open: trust, extended right at the edge of privacy, a message that could easily have been sealed shut but was not. This degree suggests that Leo's whole performance, thirty degrees of being seen, ends not with a grand final bow but with something quieter and more vulnerable, real information, offered honestly, with nothing hidden behind a sealed flap.

When a planet or point falls here

A planet at this degree often shows real willingness to leave something important open and legible rather than protected behind a formal seal. Mercury or the Moon here can indicate honesty that chooses transparency over the safety of a closed envelope. It rewards trusting that what you have written is actually fine being read.

What have you written, or said, that you are ready to leave unsealed, trusting whoever reads it with the whole truth?
