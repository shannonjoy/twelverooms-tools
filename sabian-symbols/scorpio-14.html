Scorpio 14°

The traditional symbol for this degree, one of the 360 Sabian symbols first recorded in 1925: “Linemen work high above the street, running new telephone connections between poles.”

THE SYMBOL

“Linemen work high above the street, running new telephone connections between poles.”

Scorpio 14° · Sabian symbols, devised 1925 (Marc Edmund Jones, clairvoyant Elsie Wheeler)

Remember to round up: a planet or point at 9°30′ takes the 10° symbol, not the 9° one.

What this degree describes

Literal wiring for communication, dangerous work performed overhead so that distant voices can eventually reach each other. Scorpio's gift for real, unguarded intimacy depends on exactly this kind of unglamorous infrastructure: the connection has to actually be built and maintained by someone willing to climb up and string the line, not simply wished into existence.

When a planet or point falls here

A planet here often shows real skill establishing durable channels of communication between people, or between systems. Mercury or Uranus can indicate a genuine talent for literally wiring people together across real distance.

What connection have you been meaning to actually run the line for, instead of hoping the signal reaches on its own?
