Capricorn 26°

The traditional symbol for this degree, one of the 360 Sabian symbols first recorded in 1925: “A water spirit dances in the shimmering mist thrown up by a waterfall.”

THE SYMBOL

“A water spirit dances in the shimmering mist thrown up by a waterfall.”

Capricorn 26° · Sabian symbols, devised 1925 (Marc Edmund Jones, clairvoyant Elsie Wheeler)

Remember to round up: a planet or point at 9°30′ takes the 10° symbol, not the 9° one.

What this degree describes

Even Capricorn's mountain terrain produces something this unguarded: a spirit made entirely of movement and light, dancing in exactly the spray that a hard, falling force of water throws into the air. This degree suggests that where the ground gets steepest and the water falls hardest, something genuinely magical gets generated as a byproduct, unplanned and lovely. Capricorn rarely trusts anything this ungoverned, and this degree asks it to notice the enchantment the climb itself keeps producing.

When a planet or point falls here

A planet at this degree often shows real, unexpected magic generated by difficulty itself, a lightness that appears specifically at the site of real force or hardship. Neptune here is especially at home, favoring genuine enchantment found in demanding, elemental conditions. It rewards watching for the mist your own hardest efforts have been quietly throwing off.

What unexpected lightness has your hardest, most demanding work actually been generating, right in its own spray?
