The traditional symbol for this degree, one of the 360 Sabian symbols first recorded in 1925: “A finger points to a significant passage in an open book.”

THE SYMBOL

“A finger points to a significant passage in an open book.”

Taurus 21° · Sabian symbols, devised 1925 (Marc Edmund Jones, clairvoyant Elsie Wheeler)

Remember to round up: a planet or point at 9°30′ takes the 10° symbol, not the 9° one.

What this degree describes

Someone has already done the underlining work: out of every page and paragraph available, one line has been singled out as the one that matters right now. This degree is guidance made simple and specific, not a whole library handed over, just the exact passage the moment calls for. Taurus values what is concrete and usable, and this degree delivers precisely that, meaning distilled down to one legible line instead of scattered across an entire volume.

When a planet or point falls here

A planet at this degree often shows real skill at finding, or receiving, the one relevant piece of information inside a much larger body of material. Mercury or Jupiter here can indicate a gift for teaching or research that locates exactly the passage a person actually needs. It rewards trusting the pointing finger instead of insisting on reading the whole book yourself.
